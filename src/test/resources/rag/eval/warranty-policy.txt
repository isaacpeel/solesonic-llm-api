Coverage period. The Solesonic Pro amplifier warranty covers manufacturing defects in parts for twenty four months from the date of purchase shown on the original receipt.

Labor coverage. Repair labor performed by an authorized service center is covered only during the first twelve months; after that the owner pays labor while parts remain covered.

Exclusions. Damage from liquid spills, lightning strikes, power surges, unauthorized modification and cosmetic wear on the chassis is excluded from coverage.

Claims process. To open a claim, email support with the serial number, a photo of the receipt and a short description of the fault; a return authorization number is issued within three business days.

Refunds. A full refund is available within thirty days of purchase for units returned in the original packaging; after thirty days only repair or replacement is offered.
